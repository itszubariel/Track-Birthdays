Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026 · Applies to the Track Birthdays web app and Android application.

The English version of this document is the legally binding version.

This Privacy Policy explains how Track Birthdays ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. We keep things simple: your data is used only to make the app work, never for advertising or analytics.

1 Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Track Birthdays, we collect:

  • Account information: email address, username, and password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  • Profile information: full name, profile photo, and your own birthday if you choose to provide them
  • Birthday data: names, dates, notes, and photos of people whose birthdays you add
  • Notification preferences: your preferred notification time
  • Push subscription data: browser push tokens used to deliver notifications to your devices
  • Group data: group names, colors, and avatars you create to organise birthdays
  • Birthday preferences: notification toggle, wished status, archive status, and gift status for each birthday
  • Gift preferences: interests, dislikes, and relationship to the person you send to our AI gift suggestion feature
  • Language preference: your selected app language stored locally in your browser

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the app's features:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Store and display your birthday data
  • Send push notifications about upcoming birthdays
  • Allow you to customise your profile and preferences
  • Generate AI-powered gift suggestions based on your input
  • Display the app in your preferred language
  • Export your data as .ics calendar files or .json downloads

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

3 Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database platform. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Profile photos and avatars are stored using Supabase Storage. Some preferences (such as your selected language) are stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

4 Third-Party Services

We use the following services solely to operate the app. Your data is shared with them only to the extent necessary:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, and file storage (Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare: background job scheduling for notifications (Privacy Policy)
  • Google FCM: push notification delivery for Chrome and Brave (Privacy Policy)
  • Mozilla Push Service: push notification delivery for Firefox (Privacy Policy)
  • Vercel: web hosting and serverless functions (Privacy Policy)
  • Groq: AI-powered gift suggestions; your gift preferences (interests, dislikes, relationship) are sent to Groq's API to generate ideas (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Fonts: font delivery for the app UI (Privacy Policy)

We do not sell your data to any third party.

5 Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, your browser generates a push subscription token stored in our database. This token is used exclusively to deliver birthday reminders. You can disable notifications at any time from your browser settings or profile page, or toggle them off for individual birthdays from the birthday detail view.

6 Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data, including your profile, birthday entries, and push subscriptions, is permanently deleted.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information at any time via your profile
  • Delete your account and all associated data via the profile page
  • Disable push notifications at any time
  • Export your data as .ics or .json files from the profile page

8 Children's Privacy

Track Birthdays is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10 Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at contact.trackbirthdays@gmail.com.
